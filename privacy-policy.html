About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CalcNest ("we", "our" or "us") handles information when you visit and use https://calcnest-7s6.pages.dev/. By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

CalcNest provides online calculators and educational information. You can browse most of the website without creating an account.

We may receive information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us by email. This may include your email address, name, message and any other details you choose to share.

Like many websites, certain technical information may also be collected automatically by hosting providers, security services, analytics tools or advertising partners. This may include browser type, device information, approximate location derived from an IP address, pages visited and technical activity.

2. Calculator Information

CalcNest calculators are designed to help visitors perform mathematical, financial, health, measurement and other everyday calculations.

Where a calculator performs its calculations directly in your browser, the values you enter are processed locally by that page. However, the website's hosting provider, browser, advertising services or other third-party technologies may process technical information as described in this policy.

Do not enter passwords, payment card details or other highly sensitive personal information into calculator fields.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files that websites and third-party services may store in your browser. They can support website functionality, remember preferences, measure usage and help deliver advertising.

CalcNest and its service providers may use cookies, web beacons, pixels, local storage or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Supporting website functionality and security.
  • Understanding website traffic and usage patterns.
  • Remembering certain preferences where supported.
  • Measuring advertising performance.
  • Delivering or personalizing advertisements where permitted.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising

CalcNest may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense or other advertising partners.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve advertisements based on visits to CalcNest and/or other websites on the Internet.

Users may manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Information about how Google uses data on partner websites is available at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services.

You may also visit YourAdChoices to learn about choices for interest-based advertising from participating companies.

Other advertising providers may also use cookies or similar technologies if they serve advertisements on this website. Their data practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

We may use website analytics or performance measurement services to understand general visitor activity, identify technical issues and improve our website.

These services may process information such as page views, browser and device details, referral sources and approximate geographic information. Where such services are enabled, their processing is also subject to their own privacy policies.
