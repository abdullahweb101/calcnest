How Energy Conversion Works

Energy is the capacity to do work or transfer heat. The joule is the SI unit of energy. Other units are used in electricity, nutrition, heating and particle physics.

Result = Value × Source factor ÷ Destination factor

All supported units are converted through joules as a common base unit.

Examples

1 kWh = 3,600,000 joules
1 kcal = 4,184 joules
1 Wh = 3,600 joules

Calories and food Calories

In nutrition, a food Calorie (capital C) usually means one kilocalorie (kcal), equal to 1,000 small scientific calories. This converter labels both units separately to avoid confusion.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many joules are in one kilojoule?

One kilojoule equals 1,000 joules.

How many joules are in one kilowatt-hour?

One kilowatt-hour equals exactly 3,600,000 joules.

How many calories are in one kilocalorie?

One kilocalorie equals 1,000 small calories. A food Calorie generally means one kilocalorie.

What is the SI unit of energy?

The joule (J) is the SI unit of energy.

Is kWh a unit of power?

No. A kilowatt-hour is a unit of energy. A kilowatt (kW) is a unit of power.

Is this energy converter free?

Yes. You can use it without registration or payment.
