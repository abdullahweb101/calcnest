Frequently asked questions

Can every decimal be written as a fraction?

Every terminating decimal can be written as a fraction. Repeating decimals can also be represented as fractions, but need a different conversion method.

Does this tool simplify the fraction?

Yes. It reduces the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor.
