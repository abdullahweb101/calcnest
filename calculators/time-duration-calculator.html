What Is a Time Duration Calculator?

A time duration calculator finds the amount of time between two clock readings. It is useful for calculating work shifts, study sessions, travel time, meeting lengths, exercise sessions, breaks and other activities.

This tool calculates the difference between two times and can subtract a break period to show net duration. It also supports overnight periods, where the end time occurs after midnight on the next day.

How to Use the Time Duration Calculator

  1. Enter the start time.
  2. Enter the end time.
  3. Enable the overnight option if the end time is on the following day.
  4. Enter any break duration you want to exclude.
  5. Click Calculate Duration to view the results.

Time Duration Formula

Duration = End time − Start time

Times are converted into minutes after midnight. If overnight mode is enabled and the end time is earlier than the start time, 24 hours are added to the end time before the difference is calculated.

Net duration = Gross duration − Break duration

The calculator reports net duration after the entered break. If the break is longer than the measured time, the calculator asks you to correct the input.

Examples of Time Duration

  • 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM: 8 hours.
  • 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM: 8 hours and 30 minutes.
  • 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM next day: 8 hours with overnight mode enabled.
  • 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM with a 30-minute break: 8 net hours.

What Are Decimal Hours?

Decimal hours express time as a single number rather than separate hours and minutes. They are often used in timesheets, payroll calculations, project tracking and billing.

Decimal hours = Total minutes ÷ 60

For example, 2 hours and 30 minutes equals 150 minutes. Dividing 150 by 60 gives 2.5 decimal hours. This is a mathematical conversion, not a wage or payroll calculation.

Gross Time vs. Net Time

Gross duration is the full elapsed time between the start and end. Net duration is the gross duration after subtracting breaks or other excluded periods. Whether a break is paid or unpaid depends on the employer, contract and local rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I calculate hours between two times?

Enter the start and end times. The calculator finds their difference and displays hours, minutes and seconds.

2. Can I calculate an overnight shift?

Yes. Enable the overnight option when your end time occurs on the following day, such as a shift from 10 PM to 6 AM.

3. How do I convert minutes into decimal hours?

Divide the total minutes by 60. For example, 90 minutes equals 1.5 hours.

4. Can I subtract a lunch break?

Yes. Enter the break length in minutes. The calculator deducts it from the gross duration.

5. What if my start and end times are the same?

Without overnight mode, the duration is zero. With overnight mode enabled, the calculator treats the period as 24 hours.

6. Does this calculate payroll?

No. It calculates elapsed time only. Payroll may also depend on rates, overtime rules, rounding policies and local employment laws.

7. Can I use 24-hour time?

Yes. The time fields use your browser's standard time input, commonly displayed in either 12-hour or 24-hour format depending on your device.

8. Is it free?

Yes. This CalcNest calculator is free to use without registration.
