Time conversion formulas

All supported units are converted through a common base unit: seconds. The converter first changes your input into seconds and then divides by the number of seconds in the target unit.

Value in seconds = Input value × Seconds per source unit

Converted value = Value in seconds ÷ Seconds per target unit

Understanding different time units

Milliseconds and seconds

A millisecond is one-thousandth of a second. Milliseconds are commonly used to measure computer response times, short delays, electronic signals and fast events. Seconds are a standard unit for measuring everyday durations.

Minutes and hours

Minutes and hours are widely used for meetings, lessons, travel, work schedules, exercise sessions and everyday planning. One hour contains 60 minutes, or 3,600 seconds.

Days and weeks

Days and weeks are useful for planning deadlines, projects, holidays, routines and schedules. A standard day contains 24 hours, while a week contains seven days.

Months and years

Months vary from 28 to 31 days. Years normally contain 365 days, while leap years contain 366 days. For that reason, this tool uses average Gregorian values when converting months and years into smaller units. For exact calendar-date calculations, use a date calculator instead.

Frequently asked questions

1. How many seconds are in one minute?

One minute contains exactly 60 seconds.

2. How many minutes are in one hour?

One hour contains 60 minutes, which is equal to 3,600 seconds.

3. How many seconds are in one day?

A standard 24-hour day contains 86,400 seconds.

4. How do I convert minutes into hours?

Divide the number of minutes by 60. For example, 90 minutes divided by 60 equals 1.5 hours.

5. How do I convert hours into seconds?

Multiply the number of hours by 3,600. For example, 2 hours equals 7,200 seconds.

6. How many days are in a week?

One standard week contains seven days.

7. Does this calculator use 30 days for every month?

No. It uses an average Gregorian month of 30.436875 days. Actual calendar months vary in length, so a calendar-date calculation may give a different result.

8. Does a year always have 365 days?

No. A common year has 365 days and a leap year has 366 days. This converter uses an average Gregorian year of 365.2425 days for general unit conversion.

9. Can I convert milliseconds into hours?

Yes. Select milliseconds as the starting unit and hours as the target unit. The calculator will perform the conversion automatically.

10. Is the CalcNest Time Unit Converter free?

Yes. It is free to use online and does not require registration or payment.
