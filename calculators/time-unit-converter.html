Time Conversion Formula

The converter uses seconds as its base unit. Each input value is multiplied by its unit's number of seconds, then divided by the destination unit's number of seconds.

Result = Value × Source factor ÷ Destination factor

Examples

2 hours to minutes:

2 × 60 = 120 minutes

3 days to hours:

3 × 24 = 72 hours

120 seconds to minutes:

120 ÷ 60 = 2 minutes

Important note about months and years

Calendar months have different lengths, from 28 to 31 days. This converter uses an average Gregorian month of 30.436875 days and an average Gregorian year of 365.2425 days. For an exact calendar date calculation, use a date calculator instead.

How many seconds are in one minute?

How many minutes are in an hour?
