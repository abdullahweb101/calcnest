ROI is calculated using total contributions and total value received. CAGR is an annualized estimate based on a simplified single-period model. It does not account for the exact dates of additional contributions or withdrawals. Taxes, fees and inflation are not included.

What Is Investment Return?

Investment return measures how much money an investment has gained or lost compared with the amount invested. It can be expressed as a currency amount or a percentage.

Return on Investment Formula

ROI = [(Total Value Received − Total Amount Invested) ÷ Total Amount Invested] × 100

Annualized Return Formula

CAGR = [(Ending Value ÷ Beginning Value)1 / Years − 1] × 100

Example

Suppose you invest $10,000 and your investment grows to $15,000 over five years, with no additional contributions or withdrawals. Your profit is $5,000 and your ROI is 50%. Your annualized return is approximately 8.45%.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is ROI?

Return on investment is a percentage that compares net profit or loss with the total amount invested.

2. What is a positive ROI?

A positive ROI means the total value received is greater than the amount invested.

3. What is a negative ROI?

A negative ROI indicates that the investment has lost money based on the values entered.

4. What is CAGR?

Compound annual growth rate is a smoothed annual growth rate over a period. It does not represent the actual return in every year.

5. Does this calculator include withdrawals?

Yes. Enter withdrawals received during the investment period. They are included in the total value received for the simplified ROI calculation.
