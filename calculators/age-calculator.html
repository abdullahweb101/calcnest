What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online tool that determines the time elapsed between a person's date of birth and a selected calculation date. It can show age in complete years, months and days, as well as total elapsed days and weeks.

Unlike a simple year subtraction, an exact age calculation considers whether the birthday has occurred in the current year, the number of days in each month and leap years.

How to Use This Age Calculator

  1. Choose your date of birth using the date picker.
  2. Keep the calculation date as today or select another date.
  3. Click Calculate Exact Age.
  4. View your age in years, months and days.
  5. Review your total days lived and next birthday countdown.

How Is Exact Age Calculated?

The calculator compares the birth date with the selected calculation date using calendar-based date arithmetic.

Elapsed Time = Calculation Date − Date of Birth

The result is separated into complete years, remaining months and remaining days.

Total elapsed days are calculated separately from the difference between the two calendar dates. This includes leap days where applicable.

What Does Your Age in Different Units Mean?

Age in Years

Complete years show how many full calendar years have passed since your birth date.

Age in Months

Total months represent complete calendar months between your date of birth and the selected date.

Age in Weeks

Total weeks are calculated by dividing the total elapsed days by seven. The remaining days are not included in the complete-week count.

Age in Days

Total days show the actual number of calendar days that have elapsed between the two dates.

How Leap Years Affect Age Calculations

A leap year has 366 days instead of the usual 365 because February contains 29 days. Leap years are generally years divisible by four, except century years that are not divisible by 400.

For example, 2024 was a leap year, while 2100 will not be a leap year. The calculator uses actual calendar dates, so leap days are included in elapsed-day calculations.

What Is a Next Birthday Countdown?

The next birthday countdown estimates the number of days remaining until your next birthday after the selected calculation date. If the calculation date is your birthday, the calculator reports that it is your birthday.

For a February 29 birthday in a non-leap year, this calculator uses February 28 as the birthday reference date for the countdown.

Common Uses of an Age Calculator

  • Finding exact age for personal records.
  • Checking age requirements for applications.
  • Calculating age on a historical date.
  • Planning birthday celebrations.
  • Finding the number of days between birth and today.
  • Checking age in years, months and days for forms.
Note: Age calculation conventions can differ for specific legal, administrative or cultural purposes. Use the applicable official rules when calculating age for legal eligibility or formal documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I calculate my exact age?

Enter your date of birth and the date you want to calculate your age on. The calculator compares both dates and returns complete years, months and days.

2. Does this age calculator account for leap years?

Yes. It uses calendar dates and actual elapsed days, including February 29 in leap years.

3. Can I calculate my age on a past date?

Yes. Select a past date in the Calculate Age On field to find your age on that specific date.

4. Can I calculate my age in days?

Yes. The results include the total number of elapsed days between your date of birth and the selected calculation date.

5. How are total weeks calculated?

Total complete weeks are calculated by dividing elapsed days by seven and taking the whole-number result.

6. What happens if I enter a future birth date?

The calculator will show an error because a date of birth cannot be later than the selected calculation date.

7. Is age in months the same as total months lived?

The calculator reports complete calendar months between the two dates. This may differ from dividing total days by an average month length.

8. Is this calculator free?

Yes. You can use the CalcNest age calculator without creating an account or paying a fee.
