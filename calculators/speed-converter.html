How Does Speed Conversion Work?

Speed describes how much distance an object travels in a given amount of time. Different countries and industries use different speed units. Road signs commonly use km/h or mph, while scientific calculations often use meters per second.

Speed conversion formula

Converted speed = Original speed × Source unit factor ÷ Destination unit factor

This calculator converts the entered value into meters per second as a common base unit and then calculates the destination value.

Example: km/h to mph

One kilometer per hour equals approximately 0.621371192 miles per hour.

100 km/h × 0.621371192 = 62.1371192 mph

Example: mph to km/h

One mile per hour equals exactly 1.609344 kilometers per hour.

60 mph × 1.609344 = 96.56064 km/h

Example: m/s to km/h

10 m/s × 3.6 = 36 km/h

Example: knots to km/h

20 knots × 1.852 = 37.04 km/h

Where is speed conversion useful?

  • Comparing vehicle speeds between countries.
  • Understanding road speed limits in km/h and mph.
  • Converting running, cycling and athletic speeds.
  • Working with aviation and nautical speed measurements.
  • Solving physics, engineering and classroom problems.

Speed versus velocity

Speed describes how fast an object moves, while velocity also includes its direction. This calculator converts speed magnitudes and does not calculate direction or acceleration.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert km/h to mph?

Multiply the speed in kilometers per hour by 0.621371192. For example, 100 km/h is approximately 62.14 mph.

How do I convert mph to km/h?

Multiply miles per hour by 1.609344. For example, 60 mph equals 96.56064 km/h.

How many km/h is one knot?

One international knot equals exactly 1.852 kilometers per hour.

How many meters per second are in one km/h?

One kilometer per hour equals approximately 0.2777778 meters per second.

What is the difference between mph and km/h?

Miles per hour measures distance in miles over one hour, while kilometers per hour measures distance in kilometers over one hour.

What does a knot measure?

A knot is a unit of speed equal to one nautical mile per hour. It is commonly used in maritime navigation and aviation.

What is the standard scientific speed unit?

The meter per second (m/s) is the SI unit of speed.
