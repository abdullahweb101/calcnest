What Is a Day of the Week Calculator?

A day of the week calculator identifies the weekday associated with a particular calendar date. It can tell you whether a date falls on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday or Sunday.

This tool is useful for checking historical dates, planning future events, verifying birthdays, organizing appointments, preparing schedules and understanding calendar patterns.

How to Use the Weekday Calculator

  1. Select any valid date using the date field.
  2. Click Find Day of the Week.
  3. Read the weekday displayed in the result.
  4. Review the ISO week, day number, quarter and leap-year information.

How Is the Day of the Week Determined?

Every valid calendar date corresponds to a weekday. The calculator uses the built-in JavaScript date system with UTC-based date components to avoid errors caused by local time-zone offsets.

Day of year = Number of elapsed days since January 1 + 1

The weekday is determined from the calendar date itself. Leap years are accounted for automatically, so dates after February 29 are assigned the correct weekday in leap years.

Understanding the Additional Results

ISO Week Number

The ISO week-numbering system starts each week on Monday. ISO week 1 is the week containing the first Thursday of the year. Some dates near New Year may belong to the final week of the previous ISO week-year or the first week of the next one.

Day of the Year

This is the sequential day number within the year. January 1 is day 1. December 31 is day 365 in a common year and day 366 in a leap year.

Calendar Quarter

A calendar year is commonly divided into four quarters: Q1 covers January to March, Q2 April to June, Q3 July to September and Q4 October to December.

Leap Year

A leap year has 366 days and includes February 29. A Gregorian calendar year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4, except century years must also be divisible by 400.

Leap year rule: divisible by 4, but century years must be divisible by 400.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I find what day a date was?

Select the date in the calculator and click Find Day of the Week. The result displays its weekday.

2. Can I check a date in the past?

Yes. Select a historical date supported by your browser's date input.

3. Can I check a future date?

Yes. The calculator can determine weekdays for future dates as well.

4. What is an ISO week number?

It is a week number based on the ISO week-date standard, where weeks start on Monday and week 1 contains the first Thursday of the year.

5. What does day of the year mean?

It is the date's position in its year, from 1 through 365 or 366.

6. Does it account for leap years?

Yes. The calculator applies Gregorian leap-year rules and accounts for February 29.

7. Why can an ISO week-year differ from the calendar year?

ISO weeks begin on Monday and week 1 contains the first Thursday. Therefore, a few days at the beginning or end of a calendar year can belong to a neighboring ISO week-year.

8. Is this calculator free?

Yes. It is free to use without registration.
