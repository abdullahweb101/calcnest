What Is Net Worth?

Net worth is the difference between the total value of everything you own and the total amount you owe. It is a snapshot of your financial position at a particular point in time.

Assets may include cash, bank balances, investments, retirement savings, property, vehicles and business interests. Liabilities may include mortgages, credit card balances, personal loans and other outstanding debts.

Net Worth Formula

Net worth is calculated by subtracting total liabilities from total assets. A positive result means the value of your listed assets is greater than your listed debts. A negative result means your listed liabilities exceed your assets.

How to Use This Calculator

  4. Enter the outstanding balances of your loans and other debts.

Why Track Your Net Worth?

Tracking net worth over time can help you understand whether your overall financial position is improving. It can also help you set savings goals, monitor debt reduction and review major financial decisions.

One calculation is only a snapshot. Repeating the calculation periodically using consistent valuation methods can make changes easier to understand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can net worth be negative?

Yes. Net worth is negative when total liabilities are greater than total assets. This does not by itself determine your long-term financial outlook.

Should I include my home?

Yes. You can include your home's estimated current market value as an asset and enter the remaining mortgage balance as a liability.

Should I use the original purchase price of my car?

It is generally more useful to enter an estimated current resale value rather than the original purchase price.

How often should I calculate net worth?

You may choose to review it monthly, quarterly or annually. Use a consistent approach so that comparisons are meaningful.

Does this calculator give financial advice?

No. It provides a basic calculation using your entries and does not assess your full financial circumstances.
