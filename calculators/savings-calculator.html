How Does a Savings Calculator Work?

A savings calculator projects how your balance may grow over time. It considers your starting balance, recurring monthly contributions, interest rate and compounding frequency.

Compound Growth Formula

A = P(1 + r/n)nt
  • A: Future value of an initial deposit
  • P: Initial principal
  • r: Annual nominal interest rate as a decimal
  • n: Compounding periods per year
  • t: Time in years

Regular monthly contributions are added separately at the selected deposit timing. The calculator simulates each month so that contributions earn interest according to the selected compounding schedule.

Example

Imagine starting with $5,000 and saving another $300 every month for 10 years at an annual interest rate of 5%, compounded monthly. Your final balance will include your initial deposit, all monthly contributions and the estimated interest earned.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a savings calculator?

It estimates the future value of savings based on your starting balance, regular contributions, interest rate and time period.

2. Does this calculator include monthly deposits?

Yes. Enter a monthly contribution to include recurring deposits in your savings projection.

3. What is compound interest?

Compound interest is interest earned on both your original savings and previously accumulated interest.

4. What is the difference between daily and monthly compounding?

Daily compounding calculates interest more frequently than monthly compounding. The actual difference depends on the rate and account terms.

5. Are savings projections guaranteed?

No. Actual returns depend on account terms, changing rates, fees, taxes and deposit timing.
