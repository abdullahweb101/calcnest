What Is a Healthy Weight?

A healthy weight is a body weight associated with overall health and wellbeing for an individual. It cannot be identified by a single universal number because health is influenced by several factors, including height, age, body composition, genetics, medical history, and lifestyle.

Ideal body weight formulas provide mathematical estimates based mainly on height and sex. They are sometimes used in clinical calculations, but they should not be interpreted as a personalized health goal.

Important: The average shown by this calculator is simply the arithmetic average of four formula estimates. It is not a medically validated ideal weight for every individual.

Ideal Body Weight Formulas Explained

The calculator uses four established equations. Each formula produces a different estimate because it was developed using different assumptions and mathematical coefficients.

1. Devine Formula

Men:

IBW = 50 + 2.3 × (Height in inches − 60)

Women:

IBW = 45.5 + 2.3 × (Height in inches − 60)

2. Robinson Formula

Men:

IBW = 52 + 1.9 × (Height in inches − 60)

Women:

IBW = 49 + 1.7 × (Height in inches − 60)

3. Miller Formula

Men:

IBW = 56.2 + 1.41 × (Height in inches − 60)

Women:

IBW = 53.1 + 1.36 × (Height in inches − 60)

4. Hamwi Formula

Men:

IBW = 48 + 2.7 × (Height in inches − 60)

Women:

IBW = 45.5 + 2.2 × (Height in inches − 60)

All four formulas calculate the result in kilograms when the coefficients above are used. Height is converted to inches first.

Worked Example: Healthy Weight Calculation

Consider a male individual with a height of 175 cm. This is approximately 68.90 inches.

Devine:

50 + 2.3 × (68.90 − 60) = 70.47 kg

Robinson:

52 + 1.9 × (68.90 − 60) = 68.91 kg

Miller:

56.2 + 1.41 × (68.90 − 60) = 68.75 kg

Hamwi:

48 + 2.7 × (68.90 − 60) = 72.03 kg

The estimates differ even though the same height is used. This demonstrates why formula-based ideal body weight should be treated as an approximation rather than an exact target.

What Factors Influence a Healthy Weight?

Height and Body Structure

Height is a major input in traditional ideal body weight equations. Bone structure and natural body proportions also differ between people.

Muscle Mass and Body Composition

Two people with the same height and weight may have different proportions of muscle, fat, bone, and body water.

Age and Life Stage

Health needs and body composition can change with age. Children, adolescents, pregnant individuals, and older adults may need different assessment approaches.

Medical History

Health conditions, medications, and previous medical history can influence what weight range is appropriate for an individual.

Healthy Weight Calculator vs. BMI Calculator

A BMI calculator uses height and weight to calculate body mass index. An ideal body weight calculator uses equations to estimate a reference weight based mainly on height and sex.

Neither calculation provides a complete picture of health. They are best understood as general screening or reference tools alongside other measurements and professional assessment.

Limitations of Ideal Body Weight Formulas

  • They do not directly measure body fat or muscle mass.
  • Different formulas can produce different results.
  • They may not suit athletes or people with unusual body composition.
  • They do not account fully for age, medical history, or individual health needs.
  • They should not be used alone to set a weight-loss or weight-gain target.
If you need a personal weight goal, discuss it with a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is ideal body weight?

Ideal body weight is an estimate calculated using formulas based on factors such as height and sex. It is not a universal health target.

2. Which ideal body weight formula is best?

Devine, Robinson, Miller, and Hamwi formulas use different equations. No single formula is the best for every individual.

3. Does ideal body weight depend on height?

Yes. These traditional formulas estimate ideal body weight primarily using height and sex category.

4. Is ideal body weight the same as healthy weight?

No. Formula-based ideal body weight is only an estimate. Overall health assessment considers many additional factors.

5. Can athletes use this calculator?

Athletes can view the estimates for general information, but the equations may not reflect their muscle mass or individual body composition.

6. Why do different formulas give different results?

Each equation uses different coefficients and assumptions. As a result, the estimated weight can vary even when the same height and sex category are entered.
