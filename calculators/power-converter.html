How Power Conversion Works

Power measures the rate at which energy is transferred or work is performed. The SI unit of power is the watt, equal to one joule per second.

Converted power = Value × Source factor ÷ Destination factor

This converter uses watts as the common base unit.

Examples

1 kW = 1,000 W
1 mechanical hp ≈ 745.699872 W
2 kW = 2,000 W

Power versus energy

Power and energy are related but different. Kilowatts (kW) measure the rate of energy use, while kilowatt-hours (kWh) measure the amount of energy consumed over time. For example, a 1 kW appliance running for 2 hours uses 2 kWh of energy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many watts are in one kilowatt?

One kilowatt equals exactly 1,000 watts.

How many watts are in one horsepower?

One mechanical horsepower equals approximately 745.699872 watts.

What is the difference between hp and PS?

Mechanical horsepower and metric horsepower are different definitions. One mechanical horsepower is approximately 745.7 W, while one metric horsepower is approximately 735.5 W.

What is the SI unit of power?

The watt (W) is the SI unit of power.

Is kWh a unit of power?

No. Kilowatt-hour is a unit of energy. Kilowatt is a unit of power.

Can I convert an appliance's watts into electricity cost?

This converter changes power units only. To estimate energy cost, you also need operating time and your electricity tariff.
