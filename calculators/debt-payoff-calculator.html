What Is a Debt Payoff Calculator?

A debt payoff calculator estimates how long it may take to repay an outstanding balance based on the balance, annual interest rate, and monthly payment. It can also estimate the total interest paid over the repayment period.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current outstanding debt balance.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate charged by the lender.
  3. Enter your regular monthly payment.
  4. Add any extra amount you plan to pay each month.
  5. Select a currency and calculate your estimate.

How Debt Payoff Is Calculated

Each month, interest is calculated on the remaining balance. Your payment first covers that month's interest; the rest reduces the principal. The final payment is adjusted so that it does not exceed the remaining balance plus interest.

Making additional payments can reduce the principal faster and may lower the total interest paid. The result assumes a fixed interest rate and consistent monthly payments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does paying extra reduce interest?

Usually, yes. Extra payments reduce the outstanding principal faster, which can reduce future interest charges.

What if my payment does not cover the interest?

The balance will not be paid off under the entered payment amount. You need a payment greater than the first month's interest.

Does this calculator include late fees?

No. It does not include late fees, new borrowing, variable rates, or other lender charges.

Is this an exact payoff quote?

No. It is an estimate. Your lender's payment schedule and terms determine the actual payoff amount.
