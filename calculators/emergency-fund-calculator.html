What Is an Emergency Fund?

An emergency fund is money set aside for unexpected expenses or income disruptions. It may help cover essential costs during events such as job loss, urgent repairs, or unexpected medical bills.

How Much Should You Save?

A common planning guideline is to build savings equal to three to six months of essential expenses. Your appropriate target may be higher or lower depending on income stability, dependants, insurance, debt obligations, and access to other resources.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I include entertainment expenses?

This calculator focuses on essential costs. You can include any additional expenses you believe are necessary during an emergency.

Where should I keep an emergency fund?

Many people prefer an accessible, low-risk savings account. Consider access, fees, deposit protection, and local financial rules.

Does this calculator include investment returns?

No. It assumes no interest or investment growth on your emergency savings.

Is six months always necessary?

No. The right target depends on your personal circumstances. Three to six months is a common starting guideline, not a universal rule.
