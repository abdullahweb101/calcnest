What Is a Salary Calculator?

A salary calculator converts pay from one period into equivalent earnings over other periods. It can help employees, freelancers and job seekers compare hourly, daily, weekly, monthly and annual compensation.

Annual Salary Formula

Annual Salary = Pay Per Period × Number of Periods Per Year

Hourly Salary Formula

Hourly Pay = Annual Gross Salary ÷ Annual Working Hours

Monthly Salary Formula

Monthly Pay = Annual Gross Salary ÷ 12

Example

If your monthly salary is $5,000, your annual base salary is $60,000. If you receive a $2,000 annual bonus, your total annual earnings become $62,000 before deductions.

Gross Salary vs. Net Salary

Gross salary is pay before deductions. Net salary is the amount remaining after applicable deductions. This calculator subtracts the annual deductions you enter, but it does not automatically determine your country's payroll taxes.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I convert monthly salary to annual salary?

Multiply your monthly salary by 12 to estimate annual base pay.

2. How is hourly pay calculated?

Divide annual gross pay by the number of working hours in a year.

3. Does this calculator include bonuses?

Yes. Enter an annual bonus to include it in the total annual earnings calculation.

4. What is the difference between gross and net salary?

Gross salary is before deductions, while net salary is what remains after deductions.

5. Can I calculate salary for part-time work?

Yes. Adjust working days per week, hours per day and working weeks per year to match your schedule.
