How Does Volume Conversion Work?

Volume measures the amount of three-dimensional space occupied by a substance or object. Liquid volume is commonly measured in liters, milliliters, gallons, cups and fluid ounces.

Volume conversion formula

Converted value = Original value × Source unit factor ÷ Destination unit factor

The calculator converts the entered value into liters first and then calculates the equivalent destination measurement.

Example: Liters to US gallons

One US gallon equals approximately 3.785411784 liters. To convert 10 liters into US gallons:

10 ÷ 3.785411784 = 2.64172052 US gallons

Example: Liters to milliliters

2 liters × 1,000 = 2,000 milliliters

US customary versus Imperial volume units

US customary and British imperial volume measurements are not identical. For example, a US gallon is 3.785411784 liters, while an imperial gallon is 4.54609 liters. Their fluid ounces, pints and quarts also differ.

Where is a volume converter useful?

  • Converting ingredients and liquids in cooking recipes.
  • Comparing fuel and liquid quantities in different countries.
  • Measuring water, beverages and household liquids.
  • Working with product packaging and shipping specifications.
  • Solving mathematics, chemistry and science problems.

Volume versus weight

Volume and weight are different measurements. Liters measure space occupied, while kilograms measure mass. Converting between liters and kilograms requires the substance's density. One liter of water has a mass close to one kilogram under common reference conditions, but this is not true for every liquid.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many liters are in one US gallon?

One US liquid gallon equals exactly 3.785411784 liters.

How many liters are in one imperial gallon?

One imperial gallon equals exactly 4.54609 liters.

How many milliliters are in one liter?

One liter contains exactly 1,000 milliliters.

How many milliliters are in one US cup?

One US customary cup equals approximately 236.588 milliliters. Recipe standards can vary, so check the cup definition used by your recipe.

Are US and UK fluid ounces the same?

No. A US fluid ounce is approximately 29.5735 mL, while an imperial fluid ounce is approximately 28.4131 mL.

How many cups are in one liter?

One liter is approximately 4.22675 US customary cups.

Can I convert liters into kilograms?

Not directly for every substance. You need the substance's density to convert volume into mass or weight.
