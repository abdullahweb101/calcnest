What Is a Business Days Calculator?

A business days calculator counts the weekdays within a date range while excluding non-working days. In many workplaces, a standard business week runs from Monday to Friday, while Saturday and Sunday are treated as weekends.

Business-day calculations are useful for invoice terms, delivery estimates, application processing, customer support schedules, project timelines, banking estimates and office planning. Actual working calendars can differ by country, employer and industry.

Business Days Calculation Formula

Business Days = Weekdays in the selected range − Weekday holidays

The calculator checks each calendar date in the selected range. Monday through Friday are counted as potential business days. Saturdays, Sundays and matching custom holiday dates are excluded. A holiday that falls on a weekend does not reduce the weekday count a second time.

Example

Suppose a date range includes 10 calendar dates, with two weekend dates and one weekday holiday. The result is 7 business days, assuming the start date is included.

10 calendar days − 2 weekend days − 1 weekday holiday = 7 business days

Calendar Days vs. Business Days

Calendar days count every date, including weekends and holidays. Business days generally count Monday through Friday, excluding applicable holidays. For example, a five-calendar-day period may contain only three or four weekdays depending on which day it begins.

Why Custom Holidays Matter

Public holidays are not the same everywhere. Countries, states, provinces, banks, schools and private employers can follow different holiday calendars. Adding the dates relevant to your situation makes the estimate more useful.

For an observed holiday that moves to a nearby weekday, enter the actual date your workplace observes rather than assuming the calendar date is always the day off.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What are business days?

Business days commonly mean Monday through Friday, excluding weekends and applicable holidays.

2. Does this calculator exclude Saturdays and Sundays?

Yes. Both Saturday and Sunday are excluded automatically.

3. Can I enter my country's public holidays?

Yes. Add the relevant holiday dates in YYYY-MM-DD format. The calculator does not automatically download country-specific holiday calendars.

4. Are holidays falling on weekends counted?

A holiday on Saturday or Sunday is already excluded as a weekend. It is not deducted again as a weekday holiday.

5. Is the start date included?

By default, yes. You can turn off the start-date option to begin counting from the following date.

6. Does it support different workweeks?

This version uses a Monday-to-Friday workweek. It does not support rotating shifts or workweeks with Friday and Saturday weekends.

7. Can I use it for legal or contract deadlines?

Use caution. Official deadline rules may define working days differently and may include special rules for holidays, cut-off times and local jurisdictions.

8. Is the calculator free?

Yes. You can use this CalcNest calculator without registration or payment.
