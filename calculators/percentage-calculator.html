How to Calculate a Percentage

A percentage expresses a number as a part of 100. The word percentage means "per hundred." Percentages are commonly used for discounts, taxes, exam scores, business growth, financial comparisons and everyday decisions.

1. Find a percentage of a number

To find a percentage of a number, convert the percentage into a decimal and multiply it by the number.

Result = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

Example: 20% of 150 = (20 ÷ 100) × 150 = 30.

2. Find what percent one number is of another

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Example: 25 is what percent of 100? (25 ÷ 100) × 100 = 25%.

3. Calculate percentage increase

Increase % = ((New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Example: A price rises from 100 to 125. The increase is 25, which equals a 25% increase.

4. Calculate percentage decrease

Decrease % = ((Original Value − New Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Example: A price falls from 200 to 150. The decrease is 50, which equals a 25% decrease.

5. Calculate percentage difference

Difference % = (Absolute Difference ÷ Average of the Two Values) × 100

Example: The difference between 80 and 100 is 20. Their average is 90, so the percentage difference is approximately 22.22%.

6. Calculate percentage change

Change % = ((Final Value − Initial Value) ÷ Initial Value) × 100

A positive result means an increase. A negative result means a decrease. For example, a change from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 10% of a number?

Divide the number by 10. For example, 10% of 250 is 25.

How do I calculate a percentage without a calculator?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply the result by 100. For finding a percentage of a number, divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number.

What is the difference between percentage change and percentage difference?

Percentage change compares a final value with an initial value. Percentage difference compares two values using their average as the reference.

Can a percentage be more than 100%?

Yes. A percentage can be greater than 100% when a value is more than the reference amount.

Is this percentage calculator free?

Why can't I divide by zero?

A percentage calculation that uses a zero reference or original value as its denominator is undefined. Enter a non-zero reference value for those calculations.
