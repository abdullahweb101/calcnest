How Does a Loan Calculator Work?

A loan calculator estimates how much you need to pay each month to repay a loan over a selected period. It uses the loan principal, annual interest rate and repayment term to calculate your payment.

Loan Payment Formula

M = P × [r(1 + r)n] ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]
  • M: Monthly payment
  • P: Original loan amount
  • r: Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12)
  • n: Total number of monthly payments

When the interest rate is zero, the monthly payment is simply the loan amount divided by the number of monthly payments.

Example

Suppose you borrow $25,000 at an annual interest rate of 7.5% for 5 years. The calculator estimates your monthly payment at approximately $500.95, with total interest of approximately $5,057.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a loan calculator?

A loan calculator is an online tool that estimates monthly payments, total interest and total repayment based on your loan details.

2. How is monthly loan payment calculated?

It is calculated using the loan amount, monthly interest rate and total number of monthly payments.

3. Does a longer loan term reduce monthly payments?

Generally, yes. A longer repayment term usually reduces each monthly payment but can increase the total interest paid.

4. Can I calculate a loan with zero interest?

Yes. Enter 0 as the annual interest rate. The calculator divides the principal equally across the payment months.

5. Are the results guaranteed by a lender?

No. Results are estimates. Lender fees, insurance, variable rates and other charges may change your actual repayment.
