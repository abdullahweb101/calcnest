How Does Area Conversion Work?

Area measures the size of a two-dimensional surface. It is calculated by multiplying length by width for a rectangular surface. Common area units include square meters, square feet, acres and hectares.

Area conversion formula

Converted area = Original area × Source unit factor ÷ Destination unit factor

The calculator converts the original measurement into square meters as a common base unit, then converts it into your selected destination unit.

Important: Local land measurement units

Some countries use traditional land units such as marla, kanal, bigha and other regional measurements. Their definitions can vary by location and local convention. This converter uses internationally standardized metric and imperial area units rather than assuming one universal value for regional units.

What is the difference between an acre and a hectare?

An acre is an imperial land area unit equal to 4,046.8564224 m². A hectare is a metric unit equal to 10,000 m².

How do I calculate the area of a room?

For a rectangular room, multiply its length by its width. Make sure both measurements use the same unit before multiplying.

Can I convert marla or kanal into square feet?

These traditional land units have regional variations. You should first confirm the local definition used for your property before applying a conversion.
