How Pressure Conversion Works

Pressure is force applied over a given area. The SI unit is the pascal (Pa), equal to one newton per square meter.

Converted pressure = Value × Source factor ÷ Destination factor

The calculator uses pascals as its common base unit.

Examples

1 bar = 100,000 Pa
1 PSI ≈ 0.0689476 bar
1 standard atmosphere = 101.325 kPa

Where is pressure conversion used?

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bar are in one PSI?

One PSI is approximately 0.0689476 bar.

How many PSI are in one bar?

One bar is approximately 14.5038 PSI.

What is the SI unit of pressure?

The pascal (Pa) is the SI unit of pressure.

What is standard atmospheric pressure?

One standard atmosphere is exactly 101,325 pascals.

Are torr and mmHg identical?

They are very close in common use, but their formal definitions differ slightly. This converter uses conventional mmHg and the defined torr value.

Can this converter check tyre pressure?

It converts pressure units but does not measure tyre pressure. Use a suitable pressure gauge for an actual reading.
