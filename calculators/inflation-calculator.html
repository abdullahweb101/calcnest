What Is Inflation?

Inflation is a general increase in prices over time. When prices rise, the same amount of money may buy fewer goods and services than it did previously. Inflation is commonly measured using changes in a consumer price index or another price index.

How to Use This Inflation Calculator

  1. Enter the current price or amount of money.
  2. Enter an assumed average annual inflation rate.
  3. Enter the number of years.
  4. Select your currency and calculate the estimate.

Inflation Calculation

The calculator compounds the selected annual inflation rate over the chosen number of years. It estimates the future price of something that costs the entered amount today. It also estimates the current purchasing power of that amount in future-year terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the inflation rate fixed every year?

No. Actual inflation changes over time. This calculator assumes the same annual rate for the entire period to provide an illustration.

Does this predict future inflation?

No. It is a scenario calculator, not an economic forecast. Use a rate you want to model.

What is purchasing power?

Purchasing power describes how much goods and services a given amount of money can buy. Higher prices generally reduce purchasing power.

Can inflation be negative?

Yes. A sustained fall in the general price level is called deflation. This calculator allows a negative rate for illustrative scenarios.
