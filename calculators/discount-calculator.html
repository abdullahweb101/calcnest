What Is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator helps shoppers determine how much money they save when a product or service is sold below its original price. It calculates the discount amount and the reduced sale price.

Discount Amount Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount Percentage ÷ 100)

Sale Price Formula

Sale Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Final Price Including Tax

Final Price = Sale Price + (Sale Price × Tax Rate ÷ 100)

Example

Suppose an item costs $200 and has a 25% discount. Your discount is $50 and the sale price becomes $150. If a 10% sales tax applies after the discount, the tax is $15 and the final price is $165.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I calculate a percentage discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100.

2. What is the sale price?

The sale price is the original price after subtracting the discount amount.

3. Is sales tax applied before or after discount?

This calculator applies sales tax after the discount. Actual tax rules depend on your location and the applicable regulations.

4. Can I calculate a 100% discount?

Yes. A 100% discount reduces the sale price to zero before any applicable tax.

5. Can I use this calculator for multiple items?

This calculator handles one item or one combined original price at a time. Add the prices together if the same discount applies to all items.
