Common Temperature Reference Points

These reference temperatures help you understand the relationship between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.

Water freezing and boiling reference points assume standard atmospheric pressure. Actual boiling temperature changes with pressure and altitude. Normal body temperature varies between people and circumstances.

Temperature Conversion Formulas

Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin use different zero points and scale intervals. This means temperature conversions require formulas rather than a single multiplication factor.

Celsius to Fahrenheit

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

Fahrenheit to Celsius

°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

Celsius to Kelvin

K = °C + 273.15

Kelvin to Celsius

°C = K − 273.15

Fahrenheit to Kelvin

K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15

Kelvin to Fahrenheit

°F = (K − 273.15) × 9/5 + 32

Worked example: 25°C to Fahrenheit

Multiply 25 by 9/5 and add 32:

(25 × 9/5) + 32 = 77°F

Understanding the three temperature scales

  • Celsius: Commonly used in most countries for weather, cooking and everyday temperatures.
  • Fahrenheit: Commonly used for everyday temperature reporting in the United States.
  • Kelvin: The SI base unit of thermodynamic temperature, widely used in science and physics.

What is absolute zero?

Absolute zero is 0 Kelvin, equal to -273.15°C or -459.67°F. It is the lowest possible thermodynamic temperature. Kelvin values cannot be negative in ordinary thermodynamic temperature measurements.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 and add 32. For example, 20°C equals 68°F.

How do you convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32 from Fahrenheit and multiply the result by 5/9.

What is 0°C in Fahrenheit?

Zero degrees Celsius equals 32 degrees Fahrenheit.

What is 100°C in Fahrenheit?

100°C equals 212°F.

What is room temperature in Celsius?

A commonly used room-temperature example is around 20°C to 22°C, although comfort preferences and conditions vary.

Can Kelvin have negative values?

Ordinary thermodynamic temperatures on the Kelvin scale cannot be below absolute zero, which is 0 K.

Why does Kelvin not use a degree symbol?

Kelvin is written as K, not °K. Celsius and Fahrenheit use degree symbols.
