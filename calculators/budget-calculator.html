What Is a Monthly Budget?

A monthly budget is a plan for how you will use your income during a month. It helps you organize essential expenses, manage debt, control discretionary spending and set money aside for future goals.

Monthly Budget Formula

Total Income = Take-Home Income + Other Income
Total Expenses = Sum of All Monthly Expenses
Remaining Balance = Total Income − Total Expenses

Savings Rate Formula

Savings Rate = (Remaining Balance ÷ Total Income) × 100

Common Monthly Budget Categories

  • Housing: Rent, mortgage and housing-related costs.
  • Utilities: Electricity, water, internet and phone bills.
  • Food: Groceries, meals and household food expenses.
  • Transportation: Fuel, public transport and vehicle expenses.
  • Debt: Loan payments and credit card repayments.
  • Personal: Shopping, entertainment and subscriptions.
  • Savings: Money reserved for emergencies and future goals.

How to Improve Your Monthly Budget

  • Record your income and expenses regularly.
  • Separate essential spending from optional purchases.
  • Review recurring subscriptions and unnecessary costs.
  • Set a realistic monthly savings target.
  • Keep an emergency fund for unexpected expenses.

What Is the 50/30/20 Budget Rule?

The 50/30/20 rule is a common budgeting guideline. It suggests allocating approximately 50% of after-tax income to needs, 30% to wants and 20% to savings and debt repayment. It is a flexible guideline, not a rule that works equally well for every income level or location.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I calculate my monthly budget?

Add all monthly income sources, add all monthly expenses and subtract total expenses from total income.

2. What is a good monthly savings rate?

It depends on your income, expenses and financial goals. Saving 20% is a commonly discussed target, but any sustainable amount can help build financial security.

3. What happens if expenses are higher than income?

Your budget shows a negative remaining balance. You may need to reduce expenses, increase income or review your debt and spending commitments.

4. Should I include debt payments in my budget?

Yes. Include required loan payments, credit card repayments and other regular debt obligations in your monthly expenses.

5. Is the remaining balance automatically saved?

No. It is the amount left after the expenses you entered. You need to actually reserve that money to turn it into savings.

6. Can I use this calculator with any currency?

You can select one of the available currencies. Enter all income and expenses in the same currency for a meaningful result.
