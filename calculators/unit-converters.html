Free Online Unit Conversion Tools

Unit conversion is the process of expressing a measurement in a different unit without changing the actual quantity. For example, a distance of one kilometer can also be expressed as 1,000 meters or approximately 0.621 miles.

Different countries, industries and educational systems use different measurement systems. Metric units are widely used around the world, while customary and imperial units are still common in several applications. A reliable unit converter helps you move between these systems.

How Unit Conversion Works

Most unit conversions use a fixed conversion factor. To convert a measurement from one unit to another, multiply or divide by the appropriate factor.

Converted value = Original value × Conversion factor

For example, one kilometer equals 1,000 meters. To convert 5 kilometers into meters:

5 × 1,000 = 5,000 meters

Some conversions, such as temperature, require a formula rather than a simple multiplication factor. Temperature scales have different zero points and intervals.

Metric and Imperial Measurement Systems

Metric system

The metric system uses units such as meters, grams and liters. It is based on powers of ten, making many conversions straightforward. Common metric prefixes include kilo (1,000), centi (one hundredth) and milli (one thousandth).

Imperial and US customary units

Imperial and US customary systems include units such as inches, feet, miles, pounds, ounces and gallons. Some units have similar names but different definitions between systems, particularly volume measurements.

Choosing the correct unit

Always check whether a measurement uses US customary or imperial units. For example, a US gallon and an imperial gallon are not equal. Our individual converters will identify the relevant unit definitions clearly.
