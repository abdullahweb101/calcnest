How Does Length Conversion Work?

Length conversion changes a measurement from one unit to another while preserving the same physical distance. The calculator first converts the entered value into meters, which act as the common reference unit, and then converts that value into the selected destination unit.

Length conversion formula

Result = Input value × Source unit factor ÷ Destination unit factor

For example, one foot equals exactly 0.3048 meters. To convert 10 feet into meters:

10 × 0.3048 = 3.048 meters

To convert meters into feet, divide the meter value by 0.3048. For example, 5 meters is approximately 16.4042 feet.

Metric and imperial units

The metric system uses meters and decimal-based multiples such as kilometers, centimeters and millimeters. Imperial and US customary measurements commonly use inches, feet, yards and miles. The two systems can be converted using internationally defined conversion factors.

When should you use a length converter?

  • Checking product sizes and clothing measurements.
  • Converting road distances and travel information.
  • Working with architectural plans and construction measurements.
  • Solving school, college and engineering measurement problems.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many feet are in one meter?

One meter equals approximately 3.28084 feet.

How many centimeters are in one inch?

How many kilometers are in one mile?

One international mile equals 1.609344 kilometers.

What is the difference between length and distance?

Length usually describes the size of an object or the measurement between two points. Distance commonly describes how far apart two locations are or how far something travels.
