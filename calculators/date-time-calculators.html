What Are Date & Time Calculators?

Date and time calculators are online tools that help solve common calendar and clock-related problems. They can calculate the number of days between dates, estimate working days, add or subtract time, convert timestamps and compare time zones.

These tools are useful for personal planning, business scheduling, project management, travel planning, software development, education and everyday tasks.

What Can You Calculate?

Calendar Calculations

Calculate date differences, weekdays, week numbers, leap years and days in a month.

Time Calculations

Add or subtract time, calculate durations and plan countdowns for important events.

Work & Scheduling

Estimate working days, exclude weekends and plan meetings across time zones.

How to Choose the Right Date Calculator

  • Use Date Difference Calculator to compare two calendar dates.
  • Use Business Days Calculator when weekends should be excluded.
  • Use Time Duration Calculator to find elapsed hours and minutes.
  • Use Countdown Calculator to track time until an event.
  • Use Time Zone Converter to compare times in different locations.
  • Use Calendar Generator to view a complete month or year.
