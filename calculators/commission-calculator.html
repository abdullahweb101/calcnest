What Is Sales Commission?

Sales commission is compensation based on sales made by an employee, salesperson, agent or business partner. It is often calculated as a percentage of eligible sales, although some agreements use fixed amounts or tiered rates.

How to Use This Commission Calculator

  1. Enter the total sales amount that qualifies for commission.
  2. Enter the commission percentage agreed with your employer or client.
  3. Add any fixed commission or bonus if applicable.
  4. Enter base salary for the same period if you want to estimate combined earnings.
  5. Optionally enter a sales target to calculate target achievement.

Commission Calculation

For a simple percentage-based plan, commission is calculated by multiplying eligible sales by the commission rate. Any additional fixed commission or bonus is added separately to calculate total variable commission.

Total earnings in this calculator equal base salary plus calculated commission and the additional bonus. The result is a gross estimate before taxes, deductions or other payroll adjustments.

Types of Commission Structures

Flat-rate commission: A fixed percentage is applied to all eligible sales.

Fixed commission: A set amount is paid for each sale or completed transaction.

Base salary plus commission: An employee receives a regular salary in addition to variable sales compensation.

Tiered commission: Different rates apply to different sales levels. This calculator models a single flat rate, not a tiered plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a 5% commission?

Multiply eligible sales by 0.05. For example, a 5% commission on $10,000 in eligible sales equals $500.

Is commission calculated before or after tax?

This calculator estimates gross commission before tax. Actual payroll treatment depends on local tax laws and the employment arrangement.

Can I calculate commission with a base salary?

Yes. Enter the base salary for the same period to estimate total gross earnings.

Does this support tiered commission?

No. It uses one flat commission rate. A tiered plan needs each sales band and its applicable rate to be calculated separately.

What if I do not have a sales target?

Leave the target field at zero. Commission and total earnings will still be calculated.
