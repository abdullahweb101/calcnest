How Retirement Savings Grow

Retirement savings may grow through regular contributions and investment returns over time. Starting earlier can provide more time for potential compounding, although investment returns are not guaranteed.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current age and planned retirement age.
  2. Enter your existing retirement savings.
  3. Enter the amount you expect to contribute each month.
  4. Choose an estimated annual investment return.
  5. Optionally enter an annual increase to your monthly contribution.

Calculation Method

This calculator estimates growth monthly. It assumes contributions are made at the end of each month, returns are compounded monthly, and contributions increase once per year if an annual increase is entered. It does not account for taxes, fees, inflation, or market volatility.

Frequently Asked Questions

What annual return should I use?

There is no guaranteed return. Use cautious assumptions and consider a range of possible outcomes rather than relying on one forecast.

Does the result account for inflation?

No. The result is a future nominal amount. Inflation can reduce the purchasing power of that amount.

Are investment returns guaranteed?

No. Actual returns can vary and investments can lose value.

Does this include government pensions?

No. Enter only the savings and contributions you want to model. Public pensions and employer benefits are not automatically included.
