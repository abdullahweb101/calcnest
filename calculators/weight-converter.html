How Does Weight Conversion Work?

Weight conversion changes a measurement from one unit to another. In everyday use, the terms weight and mass are often used interchangeably. Strictly speaking, mass measures the amount of matter in an object, while weight is the force caused by gravity acting on that mass.

Weight conversion formula

Converted value = Original value × Source unit factor ÷ Destination unit factor

This calculator converts the entered value into kilograms first, then calculates the equivalent value in your selected destination unit.

Example: Kilograms to pounds

One kilogram equals approximately 2.204622622 pounds. To convert 10 kilograms into pounds:

10 × 2.204622622 = 22.04622622 pounds

Example: Pounds to kilograms

One international avoirdupois pound is exactly 0.45359237 kilograms. Therefore:

150 × 0.45359237 = 68.0388555 kilograms

Metric and imperial weight units

  • Metric: milligrams, grams, kilograms and metric tonnes.
  • Imperial and US customary: ounces, pounds and stones.
  • Small measurements: milligrams and grams are common in food, science and product specifications.
  • Large measurements: kilograms and tonnes are used for cargo, industrial goods and vehicles.

Where is a weight converter useful?

People use weight conversion for fitness records, luggage allowances, cooking recipes, parcel shipping, international product listings, school assignments and manufacturing measurements.

Is a metric tonne the same as a US ton?

No. A metric tonne is 1,000 kilograms. A US short ton is 2,000 pounds, while an imperial long ton is 2,240 pounds.
