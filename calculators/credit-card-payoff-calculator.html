How Does Credit Card Interest Work?

Credit card issuers generally charge interest on balances that are not paid according to the card agreement. This calculator uses a simplified monthly interest estimate based on the APR divided by 12.

How to Use the Calculator

  1. Enter your current outstanding credit card balance.
  2. Enter the card's annual percentage rate (APR).
  3. Enter the amount you can pay each month.
  4. Add an extra payment if you plan to pay more than the regular amount.

How the Estimate Is Calculated

Monthly interest is estimated as the outstanding balance multiplied by the monthly rate. The payment is applied to interest first, and the remaining amount reduces the balance. The final payment is adjusted to clear the remaining debt.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does paying more than the minimum help?

Yes. Paying more generally reduces the principal faster and can reduce the total interest paid.

Is the APR divided by 12 an exact issuer calculation?

No. Actual card interest may use a daily periodic rate and average daily balance method. This calculator provides an estimate.

Does it include new purchases?

No. It assumes no new purchases, fees, penalties, or changes to the APR.

What if my payment is too low?

If the payment does not cover estimated monthly interest, the balance will not be repaid using the entered payment.
