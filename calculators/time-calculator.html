What Is a Time Calculator?

A time calculator performs arithmetic using hours, minutes and seconds. It can combine two durations or find the difference between them. Unlike a clock, which displays a time of day, this calculator works with elapsed durations.

It is useful for adding video or audio clip lengths, combining study sessions, tracking exercise time, calculating project durations, reviewing timesheets and solving everyday time arithmetic.

How to Use This Time Calculator

  1. Enter the hours, minutes and seconds for the first duration.
  2. Enter the second duration.
  3. Select Add or Subtract.
  4. Click Calculate Time.
  5. Review the result in hours, minutes, seconds and decimal hours.

Time Calculation Formulas

For reliable arithmetic, each duration is first converted into seconds.

Total seconds = (Hours × 3,600) + (Minutes × 60) + Seconds

For addition, the two totals are combined:

Result = First duration + Second duration

For subtraction, the second duration is deducted from the first:

Result = First duration − Second duration

The result is then converted back into hours, minutes and seconds. This calculator does not allow a negative result.

Examples of Time Addition and Subtraction

Adding two durations

2 hours 45 minutes + 1 hour 30 minutes = 4 hours 15 minutes.

Subtracting time

5 hours 20 minutes − 2 hours 10 minutes = 3 hours 10 minutes.

Converting minutes into hours

150 minutes equals 2 hours and 30 minutes, or 2.5 decimal hours.

Understanding Time Units

  • 1 minute = 60 seconds.
  • 1 hour = 60 minutes.
  • 1 hour = 3,600 seconds.
  • 1 day = 24 hours.
  • 1 day = 1,440 minutes.
  • 1 day = 86,400 seconds.

What Are Decimal Hours?

Decimal hours express a duration as a decimal number. They are useful in time tracking, project reporting and some billing systems.

Decimal hours = Total seconds ÷ 3,600

For example, 3 hours and 15 minutes equals 3.25 decimal hours. Decimal hours should not be confused with hours and minutes: 3.15 decimal hours is not the same as 3 hours and 15 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I add hours and minutes?

Enter both durations, select Add and calculate. The tool converts each value into seconds before adding them.

2. Can I subtract one duration from another?

Yes. Select Subtract. The first duration must be equal to or greater than the second duration.

3. How many seconds are in an hour?

There are 3,600 seconds in one hour because an hour contains 60 minutes and each minute contains 60 seconds.

4. Can the result be more than 24 hours?

Yes. The result displays total hours and does not reset at midnight or stop at 24 hours.

5. What is the difference between decimal hours and hours and minutes?

Hours and minutes use base-60 time units. Decimal hours use a fraction of 100 in the displayed decimal number. For example, 1 hour 30 minutes is 1.5 decimal hours.

6. Can I enter more than 59 minutes?

Enter minutes from 0 to 59 in the minutes field. If you have a larger number of minutes, convert the extra minutes into hours first.

7. Does this calculate a clock time?

No. It adds or subtracts durations. To calculate the elapsed time between two clock readings, use the Time Duration Calculator.

8. Is the calculator free?

Yes. It is free to use without registration.
