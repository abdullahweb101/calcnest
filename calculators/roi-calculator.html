What Is Return on Investment (ROI)?

Return on investment (ROI) is a performance measure used to evaluate the gain or loss generated by an investment relative to its cost. It is commonly expressed as a percentage.

ROI can help compare investment outcomes, assess business projects and understand whether an investment produced a positive or negative return. However, ROI alone does not show the full risk or timing of an investment.

How to Use This ROI Calculator

  1. Enter the original amount invested.
  2. Enter additional investment costs or contributions.
  3. Enter the final value of the investment.
  4. Add any separate income or cash received from the investment.
  5. Enter the investment period to estimate annualized return.
  6. Select your currency and calculate the result.

Understanding the Results

Total invested amount includes the initial investment and additional costs or contributions entered. Total value received includes the final investment value and additional income received.

Net profit or loss is the difference between total value received and total invested amount. ROI expresses that result as a percentage of the total invested amount.

ROI vs. Annualized Return

ROI describes the overall percentage gain or loss over the investment period. Annualized return estimates the compound yearly rate that would produce the same overall result over the entered number of years.

For investments involving multiple contributions or withdrawals at different dates, a simple annualized calculation may not accurately measure performance. A cash-flow-based method such as XIRR or money-weighted return may be more appropriate when transaction dates and amounts are available.

Important Limitations

This calculator does not automatically account for taxes, inflation, fees not entered by the user, changing market values or the timing of additional contributions. Enter all relevant costs and cash receipts consistently when using the estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good ROI?

There is no universal good ROI. It depends on the investment type, risk, time period, alternative opportunities, costs and market conditions.

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. A negative ROI means the total value received is less than the total amount invested under the values entered.

Is ROI the same as profit?

No. Profit is an amount of money, while ROI expresses profit or loss relative to the invested amount as a percentage.

Does this calculator include dividends or rental income?

You can enter additional cash income in the additional income field. Make sure not to count the same income twice if it is already included in the final value.

Does annualized return account for extra contributions?

This calculator uses a simplified total-value annualization. It does not account for the exact dates of extra contributions or cash receipts. For multiple dated cash flows, use an XIRR or money-weighted return method.
