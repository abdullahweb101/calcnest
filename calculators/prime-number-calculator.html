How it works

A prime number is an integer greater than 1 with exactly two positive factors: 1 and itself. To test n, check whether any integer from 2 through √n divides it evenly.

Quick example

29 is prime because its only positive factors are 1 and 29. 28 is composite because it has factors 1, 2, 4, 7, 14 and 28.

Frequently asked questions

Is 1 a prime number?

No. A prime number must have exactly two distinct positive factors. The number 1 has only one.

Is 2 prime?

Yes. Two is the only even prime number.
