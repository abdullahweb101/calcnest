What Is a Date Difference Calculator?

A date difference calculator finds the amount of time between two calendar dates. Instead of manually counting days on a calendar, you can enter a starting date and an ending date to get an instant result.

How Is the Difference Between Two Dates Calculated?

The calculator first identifies the earlier and later dates. It then calculates the calendar years, remaining calendar months and remaining days. This approach accounts for different month lengths and leap years.

1. Total Days

Total elapsed days = Later date − Earlier date

The standard calculation measures the number of days that have elapsed from the start date to the end date. It does not count the starting calendar date as an additional day.

2. Calendar Difference

Calendar difference is shown as completed years, followed by completed months and remaining days. For example, a date interval might be 2 years, 3 months and 10 days.

Calendar months are not converted into a fixed number of days because months have different lengths. February, for example, can have 28 or 29 days, while other months have 30 or 31 days.

3. Weeks and Remaining Days

Complete weeks = Total days ÷ 7 (rounded down)
Remaining days = Total days − (Complete weeks × 7)

4. Inclusive Date Counting

Inclusive calendar days = Total elapsed days + 1

Inclusive counting includes both the start date and the end date. This is often useful for event schedules, leave periods and date ranges. The inclusive result is one day higher than the elapsed-day result when the two selected dates are the same or different.

5. Working Days

Working days in this calculator are weekdays from Monday to Friday. Saturdays and Sundays are excluded. Both selected dates are considered when they fall on weekdays. Public holidays are not automatically removed.

Important Things to Know About Date Calculations

  • Leap years: A leap year has 366 days and includes February 29.
  • Different month lengths: Months can contain 28, 29, 30 or 31 days.
  • Inclusive counting: Including both dates adds one calendar day to the standard elapsed-day result.
  • Working days: The weekday result excludes weekends but does not automatically account for national or public holidays.
  • Time zones: This tool compares calendar dates, not exact timestamps in different time zones.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I calculate the difference between two dates?

Enter the starting date and ending date, then click Calculate. The calculator displays the calendar difference and total elapsed time in several units.

2. Does this calculator include the start date?

By default, it calculates elapsed days without adding the start date as an extra day. Enable the inclusive counting option to include both the start and end dates.

3. Can I calculate the difference between dates in different years?

Yes. You can select dates from different months and years. The calculator also accounts for leap years and different calendar month lengths.

4. How do I calculate weeks between two dates?

Divide the total elapsed days by seven. The calculator shows complete weeks and the remaining days.

5. Does the calculator count Saturdays and Sundays as working days?

No. The working-day result counts Monday through Friday. Saturdays and Sundays are excluded.

6. Are public holidays excluded from working days?

No. Public holidays are not automatically excluded because holiday calendars differ by country, region and organization.

7. What happens if I enter the end date before the start date?

The calculator automatically identifies the earlier and later dates and calculates the absolute difference between them.
