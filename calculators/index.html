How to choose the right math calculator

Use the percentage calculator for percentage-based questions, fraction tools for parts of a whole, the ratio calculator for proportional relationships, and the average calculator for summarising a data set. Prime, GCD and LCM tools help with integer properties, while scientific notation and rounding tools help express numbers clearly.

Each calculator runs in your browser. Check your inputs and review the explanation before relying on results for important work.
