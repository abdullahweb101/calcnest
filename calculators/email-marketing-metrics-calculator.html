What Are Email Marketing Metrics?

Email marketing metrics are measurements used to evaluate how recipients interact with email campaigns. They help marketers understand delivery, engagement, audience response, conversions and campaign revenue.

A useful email report looks beyond opens. Clicks, conversions, revenue, unsubscribe activity and complaints provide additional information about campaign performance.

Email Marketing Formulas

1. Email Delivery Rate

Delivery Rate = (Delivered Emails ÷ Emails Sent) × 100

2. Email Bounce Rate

Bounce Rate = (Bounced Emails ÷ Emails Sent) × 100

3. Email Open Rate

Open Rate = (Unique Opens ÷ Delivered Emails) × 100

4. Email Click-Through Rate (CTR)

Email CTR = (Unique Clickers ÷ Delivered Emails) × 100

5. Click-to-Open Rate (CTOR)

CTOR = (Unique Clickers ÷ Unique Opens) × 100

6. Email Conversion Rate

Conversion Rate = (Conversions ÷ Delivered Emails) × 100

7. Unsubscribe Rate

Unsubscribe Rate = (Unsubscribes ÷ Delivered Emails) × 100

8. Spam Complaint Rate

Complaint Rate = (Spam Complaints ÷ Delivered Emails) × 100

9. Revenue Per Delivered Email

Revenue Per Email = Attributed Revenue ÷ Delivered Emails

10. Email Campaign ROAS

Email ROAS = Attributed Revenue ÷ Campaign Cost

11. Click-to-Conversion Rate

Click Conversion Rate = (Conversions ÷ Unique Clickers) × 100

Understanding the Most Important Email Metrics

Open Rate

Open rate indicates the proportion of delivered recipients whose email clients recorded an open. It can help identify changes in subject-line engagement, but it is not a perfect measurement of human attention.

Click-Through Rate

CTR measures the share of delivered recipients who clicked at least one tracked link. It is useful for understanding whether the email content and call to action encouraged further action.

Click-to-Open Rate

CTOR compares clickers with recorded openers. It can provide a view of how effectively the email content encouraged clicks among people whose clients recorded an open. Because open tracking can be unreliable, CTOR should be interpreted cautiously.

Bounce Rate

A bounce happens when an email cannot be delivered. Hard bounces generally indicate permanent problems, such as invalid addresses. Soft bounces may result from temporary conditions such as a full inbox or a receiving server issue.

Unsubscribe Rate

Unsubscribe rate measures how many delivered recipients opted out. A sudden increase can be a reason to review audience targeting, email frequency, relevance and subscription expectations.

Conversion Rate

Conversion rate connects email activity with a defined business action. A conversion might be a completed purchase, registration, booking, download or lead form submission. The chosen attribution window should be consistent when comparing campaigns.

How to Improve Email Campaign Performance

  1. Build permission-based lists: Send to people who have agreed to receive your emails.
  2. Maintain list hygiene: Remove invalid addresses and manage repeated delivery failures.
  3. Write relevant subject lines: Make the purpose of each email clear without misleading recipients.
  4. Segment your audience: Send content that matches customer interests and lifecycle stages.
  5. Use clear calls to action: Help readers understand the next step.
  6. Optimize for mobile: Check layout, readability, links and buttons on smaller screens.
  7. Test responsibly: Compare subject lines, content and send timing with controlled experiments.
  8. Measure meaningful conversions: Connect campaigns to purchases, registrations or other business outcomes.
  9. Monitor complaints and unsubscribes: Investigate unusual changes rather than focusing only on opens.
  10. Respect consent and privacy: Follow applicable email marketing, data protection and unsubscribe requirements.

Why Open Rate Data Can Be Misleading

Many email platforms estimate opens through tracking pixels. Some email clients automatically load images or use privacy features that affect tracking. Security systems may also scan messages. As a result, recorded opens may not accurately represent actual human reading behavior.

Practical recommendation:

Use open rate as a directional metric rather than the only measure of campaign success. Give meaningful attention to unique clicks, completed conversions, revenue, complaints and unsubscribe activity.

Email Marketing Metrics vs Email Marketing ROI

Email metrics describe campaign activity and outcomes. A complete return-on-investment calculation requires a broader view of costs, including software, staff, creative production and other relevant expenses. Revenue divided by campaign cost is ROAS, not necessarily profit-based ROI.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is email open rate?

Open rate is generally unique opens divided by delivered emails, multiplied by 100. Privacy protections and automated image loading can affect its accuracy.

2. What is a good email open rate?

There is no universal benchmark. Results depend on industry, audience, email type, privacy settings and tracking methodology. Compare campaigns with similar audiences and measurement rules.

3. What is email click-through rate?

Email CTR is usually unique clickers divided by delivered emails, multiplied by 100. It measures the percentage of delivered recipients who clicked at least one tracked link.

4. What is the difference between CTR and CTOR?

CTR compares unique clickers with delivered emails. CTOR compares unique clickers with unique opens. They answer different questions about engagement.

5. How do I calculate email bounce rate?

Divide bounced messages by attempted messages and multiply by 100. Include hard and soft bounces consistently in the total.

6. What is email unsubscribe rate?

It is generally the number of unsubscribes divided by delivered emails, multiplied by 100. Always use a consistent reporting definition.

7. How is email conversion rate calculated?

Divide the number of attributed conversions by delivered emails and multiply by 100. A conversion should be clearly defined, such as a purchase or registration.

8. Why can email open rates be inaccurate?

Privacy features, automatic image loading, tracking restrictions and automated security scanning can influence recorded opens.

9. Should I use unique clicks or total clicks?

Use unique clickers for the percentage of recipients who clicked. Total clicks can help show repeated interaction with campaign links.

10. Is email ROAS the same as email ROI?

No. ROAS is attributed revenue divided by advertising or campaign spend. ROI usually considers profit or net return after relevant costs.
